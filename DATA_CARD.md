# Data Card

## Synthetic demonstration
`data/sample/synthetic_demo.npz` is generated by `make_demo_data.py`.

It is intentionally artificial and exists to verify:
1. data loading
2. training
3. inference
4. visualization
5. group-held-out evaluation

It is not a physical seismic forward model and should not be interpreted as geological evidence.

## Real data
For real experiments, keep raw third-party seismic files outside the repository unless redistribution is explicitly allowed. Record:
- source and citation
- licence/terms
- acquisition area or survey name
- sampling interval
- coordinate convention
- processing history, if known
- label source and interpreter information
- train/validation/test survey IDs

## Leakage control
Do not split adjacent slices from one seismic volume across train and test. Use volume/survey-level grouping.
